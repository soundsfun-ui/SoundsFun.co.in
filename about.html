About Sounds Fun Audio

We bring your audio to life with precision, creativity, and just the right amount of fun.

Our Mission

Headquartered in Mumbai and serving clients worldwide, we partner with artists, filmmakers, studios, and content creators who care deeply about the power of sound. From indie music to immersive film soundtracks, we work across the entire spectrum of audio production to ensure your sound is not just clean, but unforgettable.

Meet the Founder

Sarang Gupta

As the founder and lead engineer, Sarang combines technical precision with a musician's soul. With a background in both performance and production, he started Sounds Fun to create a space where artists feel understood and their sonic vision is brought to life with passion and expertise.

Our Philosophy: Sound is Story

Builds Emotion

Sound connects faster and more directly than visuals, creating an instant emotional bond with the audience.

Creates Worlds

Through careful design, audio creates immersive worlds that listeners can step into, feel, and explore.

Shapes Meaning

The strategic use of voice, music, and silence can fundamentally shape and deepen the meaning of a story.
